The sum of all tiles raises your fire rate, and your highest tile decides your gun. When the bag is full you can't pick up loot.
Hammers (yellow drops, max 3): click a tile to smash it, or press Space → arrows → Space (time slows down).
If your bag locks up with no hammer, you have 5 seconds to find one before it's game over.
Mobile: hold your finger in the arena to move, swipe on the bag, tap a tile to smash it.
